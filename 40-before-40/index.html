02 · Lift a certain amount

Bodyweight: 118 lbs. Targets set as multiples of bodyweight:

LiftTarget rangeIn lbs
Deadlift (primary)1–1.25x bodyweight118–148 lbs
Squat (secondary)0.75–1x bodyweight89–118 lbs
Bench press0.5–0.6x bodyweight59–71 lbs
Overhead press0.35–0.4x bodyweight41–47 lbs

Headline goal: deadlift 135 lbs. Secondary: squat 95–100 lbs.

  • Phase 1, weeks 1–4 (Sept 2026): 2–3 trainer sessions to groove safe deadlift and squat form and establish real tested starting numbers (not estimates) for all four lifts.
  • Phase 2, weeks 5–12 (Oct–Nov 2026): 2 lifting sessions/week. Progressive overload — add 5 lbs to deadlift and squat every 1–2 sessions as long as form holds (video-check every session), smaller 2.5 lb jumps on bench/OHP. Runs in parallel with the push-up progression, since pressing strength compounds both.
  • Phase 3, weeks 13–20 (Dec 2026–Jan 2027): Slower, more deliberate progression as weights get heavier — expect smaller jumps and occasional plateau weeks; take a 1–2 week deload if form starts breaking down. Target hitting 135 lbs on deadlift by the end of this phase.
  • Phase 4 (Feb 2027): Dedicated testing session (with the trainer, if still working together) to attempt and confirm the 135 lb deadlift as an official max, plus record final numbers on the three secondary lifts.

Realistic total timeline: 4–6 months from a September 2026 start, landing the confirmed max around Jan–Feb 2027 — well ahead of the birthday, with room to push further if it comes easily.

05 · Sleep routine, 1 month

November 1–30, 2026 — every day this month, with the exception of any flight days (none currently booked in November; if travel lands in this window, those travel days are the exception).

Target: phone away by 10:00pm, lights out by 10:30pm, for a full 8 hours before a consistent wake time (adjust these clock times if 10:30/6:30 isn’t the right window).

Evening wind-down (roughly 9:30–10:30pm):

  • A/C turned on early enough that the room is already cold by bedtime — on a timer or switched on right after dinner, not right at 10:30
  • Push-ups and plank done earlier in the day, not inside the wind-down window — both are activating
  • Medication on a consistent daily schedule — same time every day, held as tightly as the sleep window itself
  • Phone away by 10:00pm — physically out of the bedroom, not just face-down on the nightstand
  • Hair mask and eye mask as the last physical steps before lights-out

Sleep environment: room pre-cooled via the A/C, eye mask on, consistent wake time including weekends — the piece most routines skip, and the one that actually locks it in.

Also worth adding: an early-to-mid afternoon caffeine cutoff (caffeine's half-life means it's still partly active hours later), and optionally a sleep-tracking app or wearable to see whether the routine is actually producing more/better sleep.

Success metric: every day in November (flight days excepted), phone away by 10pm and lights out by 10:30pm.

06 · Plank challenge

September 14 – October 11, 2026 (moved up so it finishes before New Orleans, no travel overlap). Habit-stacked onto teeth brushing, twice a day: bodyweight squats for the duration of brushing (~2 minutes), immediately followed by one plank hold — morning and night.

  • Week 1 (Sept 14–20): squats through brushing + 60 sec plank hold, AM and PM — form check: flat back, no hip sag, glutes engaged, shoulders down
  • Week 2 (Sept 21–27): squats through brushing + 75 sec plank hold, AM and PM
  • Week 3 (Sept 28–Oct 4): squats through brushing + 90–100 sec plank hold, AM and PM
  • Week 4 (Oct 5–11): squats through brushing + push toward a 2-minute+ single hold, AM and PM

Tool: phone video or mirror to self-check squat and plank form occasionally; a structured app like “Plank Workout” if a gamified day-by-day plan helps.

07 · 40 push-ups in a row

Starting point: ~2 standard, ~15 on knees. Realistic timeline: roughly 20 weeks, starting Saturday, Sept 12, 2026.

  • Phase 1, weeks 1–4 (Sept 12–Oct 9): build knee push-ups to 25–30/set; add incline push-ups 2x/week, 3 sets of 8–10
  • Phase 2, weeks 5–10 (Oct 10–Nov 20): standard push-ups in small sets of 3–5 reps, 5–6 sets per session; lower incline height every 2 weeks; target 8–10 in a single set by end of phase
  • Phase 3, weeks 11–16 (Nov 21–Jan 1): volume building toward 15–20 in a single set — runs parallel with the lifting program, which directly compounds chest/tricep/shoulder strength
  • Phase 4, weeks 17+ (Jan 2–Feb 2027, in Mexico): max-effort testing every 1–2 weeks, targeting 40 consecutive by end of February 2027

08 · Find a therapist I like

Active search, Sept 9 – mid-October 2026. Hard requirement: some part of the cost has to be covered by health insurance — either in-network, or an out-of-network plan with reimbursement.

  • Week 1 (Sept 9–15): Write down what you actually want out of therapy — specific enough to match against a provider's specialty. Log into your insurer's member portal and find the in-network behavioral health directory; separately confirm whether your plan reimburses out-of-network mental health visits and at what rate. Note whether teletherapy is covered — it widens the pool considerably.
  • Weeks 2–3 (Sept 16–29): Search psychologytoday.com and goodtherapy.org, filtered specifically by your insurance and the issues from Week 1. Build a shortlist of 4–5 candidates. Note credential type as you go — LCSW for practical/life-situation work, MFT for relationship-pattern work, PhD/PsyD if something more specialized (like ADHD-specific work) is relevant.
  • Week 4 (Sept 30–Oct 6): Request brief consultations (often free, ~15 min) with each shortlisted candidate. Ask about their experience with your specific issues, their approach, and expected timeline for seeing progress.
  • Week 5 (Oct 7–13): Pick one. Verify the insurance/reimbursement math actually works before the first paid session — confirm coverage directly with the provider's office, not just the directory listing.
  • Ongoing after that: Commit to a 3–5 session trial before deciding whether it's the right fit. If not, cycle back to the shortlist rather than starting the whole search over.
